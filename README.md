# Net Revenue Management Analysis — Shampoo Category

FMCG Net Revenue Management case study analyzing the shampoo category using Microsoft Excel.

## Project Overview

This case study applies **Net Revenue Management (NRM)** principles to an FMCG shampoo category.

**Role:** Category Manager  
**Client:** HealthMax  
**Category:** Shampoo / FMCG  
**Primary Tool:** Microsoft Excel

The objective was to identify opportunities to improve revenue and commercial performance through market analysis, pricing and mix, profitability, new product opportunities, promotion management, forecasting, and revenue impact analysis.

## Business Questions

The analysis addresses key commercial questions:

- How is the shampoo category performing?
- Which subcategories present the strongest growth opportunities?
- Which products contribute most to revenue and profitability?
- How can pricing and pack-size architecture support growth?
- Which promotional strategy provides the strongest return?
- What is the expected 2024 revenue outlook?
- What is the potential revenue impact of the identified NRM initiatives?

## NRM Analysis Framework

The project covers the following areas:

## Key Findings

### Category Growth

Organic shampoo was the fastest-growing analyzed subcategory, with **275.82% growth from 2018 to 2022**.

This identified Organic as the strongest category expansion opportunity.

### Profitability

The profitability analysis evaluated products using:

- Gross Profit per Unit
- Gross Profit per Product
- Gross Margin
- Net Sales Contribution

This allowed product performance to be assessed using both revenue scale and profitability.

### New Category Opportunity

Organic shampoo was selected as the key category opportunity.

- March 2023 MAT Organic Units: **850,749**
- Estimated 2024 Organic Units: **1,020,899**
- Modeled organic launch contribution to the revenue bridge: **$336.9K**

### New Product Launch

A **50ml shampoo format** was modeled as a pack-price opportunity.

**Starbust Ultra Soft 50ml:**

- Net Price: **$2.30**
- COGS: **$0.70**
- Gross Profit per Unit: **$1.60**
- Gross Margin: **69.57%**
- Modeled Net Sales Contribution: **$266.0K**

### Promotion Management

Three promotional periods were evaluated against a non-promotion baseline.

The **Buy 2, Get 20% Off** promotion produced the strongest ROI at **11.84%**.

The analysis also showed that the promotion generating the largest absolute sales uplift did not necessarily generate the strongest ROI.

### Forecasting

The Excel Forecast Sheet produced:

| Year | Net Sales |
|---|---:|
| 2022 | $20.48M |
| 2023 Estimated | $20.61M |
| 2024 Baseline | $20.81M |

### Revenue Waterfall

The modeled revenue bridge includes:

- Natural Growth: **$202.7K**
- Organic Shampoo: **$336.9K**
- 50ml Shampoo: **$266.0K**

This results in estimated 2024 net sales of:

**$21.41M**

## Recommendations

1. **Prioritize organic shampoo expansion** based on its 275.82% historical growth and modeled contribution to the revenue bridge.
2. **Evaluate the 50ml entry pack** as a potential incremental revenue and margin opportunity.
3. **Optimize promotions using ROI**, rather than evaluating promotions based only on sales uplift.
4. **Manage revenue and profitability together** when prioritizing products and portfolio actions.
5. **Track actual performance against the modeled revenue bridge** to evaluate realization of the identified opportunities.
